# L3: Metrics, rules, and axis policy

L3 is the layer where systems decide what counts as reality.

If this choice is implicit, control degrades quickly.
If this choice is explicit, L3 becomes a stabilizer.

Main risk:

> Goodhart mode: optimizing a sensor while degrading the underlying dynamics.

## Typical failures

- dashboard growth with declining field outcomes,
- incentives that reward imitation over repair,
- obligation growth detached from real capacity,
- hidden transfer of error cost to lower layers,
- post-hoc metric redefinition to avoid accountability.

## Core operators

- `L3-C1`: keep metric as sensor, never as standalone objective,
- `L3-C4`: enforce stop-rule guardrails,
- `L3-C5`: return error cost to source,
- `L3-C6`: break report theater and reconnect report to field reality,
- `L3-C7`: define axis policy explicitly (which projections govern decisions).

## Readout panel

- target metric plus at least two independent counter-signals,
- escalation/refinement pressure over time,
- transferability and robustness under perturbation,
- mismatch rate between report claims and trace-level evidence.

L3 quality is measured by controllability, not by dashboard aesthetics.

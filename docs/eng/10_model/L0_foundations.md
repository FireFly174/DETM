# L0 Foundations: Distinguishability, Time and Emergence in DETM

**Status:** Canonical

This document fixes the foundational assumptions of the DETM model regarding the L0 level, the origin of time, dynamics, and the emergent nature of space. The statements below are considered closed and are not subject to further discussion unless the core model is revised.

---

## 1. Rejection of Primitive Space, Coordinates, and Quantities

Within DETM, space, coordinates, distances, fields, and numerical quantities are **not primitive entities**.

Any apparent “point of space” is defined **only through its projection onto other points**, i.e., through the degrees of freedom by which it is distinguishable from its neighborhood.

Consequences:

* Coordinates are derivative.
* Metrics are derivative.
* Fields are aggregated representations.
* Quantities are products of coarse-graining.

There is no notion of a point existing independently of its relations.

---

## 2. The L0 Limit

At the L0 level, the system admits only a single distinguishable statement:

```
E ∈ {0, 1}
```

Interpreted strictly as:

> energy present / energy absent

At L0:

* no numerical values exist;
* no coordinates exist;
* no directions exist;
* no fields exist;
* no geometry exists.

L0 is the level of **pure distinguishability**, not physical representation.

---

## 3. A Single Point Is Not a System

A single L0 point:

* cannot be compared;
* cannot change;
* cannot encode order;
* cannot define dynamics;
* cannot define time.

Therefore:

> A single point does not form an observable system.

Observation requires relational structure.

---

## 4. Minimal Condition for the Emergence of Time

With the introduction of **two L0 points**, the system gains:

* distinguishable configurations;
* the possibility of configuration change;
* an ordering of changes.

This ordering defines **time**.

Time in DETM is **not a coordinate or axis**. It is:

> the order of distinguishable configurations of the system.

Thus:

* time is the **first emergent dimension**;
* space emerges later as a structured aggregation of temporal relations.

---

## 5. Dynamics as the Primary Invariant

A sequence of `{0,1}` configurations over time constitutes **dynamics**.

Dynamics is:

* the minimal non-trivial invariant;
* the first carrier of structure;
* the source of all higher-level constructs (objects, fields, geometry).

There is no static ontology prior to dynamics.

---

## 6. Space as a Result of Coarse-Graining

Space in DETM is:

* not assumed;
* not predefined;
* not axiomatized.

It emerges as:

> a stable coarse-graining of distinguishability relations within dynamics.

Geometry, distance, and direction are representational languages of higher levels, not fundamental properties.

---

## 7. Bidirectional Scaling

Scaling in DETM operates symmetrically:

* **Downward refinement**:

  * quantities vanish;
  * representation collapses to binary states;
  * the system approaches L0.

* **Upward coarse-graining**:

  * distinguishability aggregates;
  * fields emerge;
  * geometry stabilizes.

Neither direction is more fundamental than the other.

---

## 8. Status of Holography

Holography is **not a principle** in DETM.

It is a consequence of representation:

> When volume is not primitive, dynamics localizes on boundaries of distinguishability.

Accordingly:

* volume acts as memory;
* boundaries act as computation;
* centers are aggregates of boundary reactions.

Holography is therefore a **mode of representability**, not an axiom.

---

## 9. Canonical Summary

> DETM contains no primitive space or time.
>
> Only distinguishability exists.
>
> Time is the ordering of distinguishable configurations.
>
> Space is a stable coarse-graining of distinguishability.
>
> Dynamics is the minimal invariant from which all higher structures emerge.

---

## 10. Closure Statement

This document closes the foundational discussion of L0, time, and space within DETM.

Any further interpretation must be expressed in terms of higher-level representations and must not contradict the statements fixed herein.

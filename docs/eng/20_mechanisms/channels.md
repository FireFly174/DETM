# Channels

Note: first-pass translation of `docs/rus/20_mechanisms/channels.md`.

Channels are structured pathways for transport/interaction that emerge from the field dynamics.

Typical questions:
- how channels form under local rules
- how stable/robust they are
- whether they increase throughput and separability of invariant structures

This document collects hypotheses and experiment hooks related to “channels”.

See also: `docs/eng/20_mechanisms/parameter_fields.md`.
